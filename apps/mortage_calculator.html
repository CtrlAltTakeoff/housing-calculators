Hardcoded Assumptions

  • Property tax is based on assessed home value, which increases by 1% per year.
  • Home insurance increases by 2% per year due to inflation.
  • Monthly HOA increases by 2% per year due to inflation.
  • These annual growth rates are fixed assumptions in this version and are not user inputs.
