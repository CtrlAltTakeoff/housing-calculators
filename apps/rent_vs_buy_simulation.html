Rent vs Buy Net Worth Simulator

Compare yearly net worth for renting versus buying under the same starting cash and assumptions. The model runs on monthly cash flow and monthly investment growth, with yearly charted outputs. Annual maintenance, home insurance, and HOA are assumed to increase by 2% per year due to inflation. Buy-side net worth assumes a 5% selling closing cost when the home is sold, and the homeowner plot can be switched to inspect key buy-side costs and values over time.

Simulation updates automatically when inputs are valid.
