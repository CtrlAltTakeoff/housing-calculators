Front-End and Back-End Ratio Explained

The front-end ratio measures how much of gross monthly income can go to housing costs. In this calculator, housing cost includes principal and interest, property tax, HOA, insurance, maintenance and repairs, and PMI when applicable.

Front-end ratio = monthly housing cost / gross monthly income

The back-end ratio measures how much of gross monthly income can go to total debt. Total debt includes housing costs plus recurring non-housing obligations such as car loans, student loans, and credit cards.

Back-end ratio = (monthly housing cost + monthly non-housing debt) / gross monthly income

In income-based mode, the calculator finds the maximum home price that stays within the selected lender rule. For example, a conventional 28/36 rule means housing should stay at or below 28% of gross monthly income, and all monthly debt together should stay at or below 36%.
